# Língua Presa

A team word game for 4 to 10 people, each on their own phone, in the same room (people talk out loud).

## Teams

The host picks in the lobby how the table splits, and the teams are drawn when the match starts:

- **Two teams**: any number of people from 4 to 10.
- **Pairs**: an even number (4, 6, 8 or 10).
- **Trios**: 6 or 9 people.

## A turn

- Teams take turns. Inside a team the narrator rotates, so everyone narrates the same number of times (1 or 2 laps, the host's choice).
- The narrator sees a **word** and **five forbidden words**, and makes their team say the word out loud without saying any forbidden word (or the word itself). The team never sees the card.
- The clock is the server's (45, 60 or 90 seconds, the host's choice).
- Narrator: **Acertou** (+1 point for the team) or **Pular** (up to 3 skips per turn, no cost).
- The other teams see the card too. If they hear a forbidden word they tap **Proibida!**: the card is dropped and the team loses 1 point.
- When the clock runs out everyone sees the cards of the turn, then someone taps to move on.

The team with the most points at the end wins; ties are shared.

## What the server hides

The card on the table is only sent to the narrator and to the teams that are not playing. Between turns nobody has a card. A late tap on an old card is refused (each card has a number).

## Bots

A bot can narrate: it starts, plays a few cards (mostly hits) and ends its turn. Bots never buzz and never move the table to the next turn.
